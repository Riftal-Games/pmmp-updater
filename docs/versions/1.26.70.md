## Encoding conventions used below

Read from the package, which works against the 2225 client:
- A field not in `required` and with no `default` is a presence-prefixed optional: `PlaySoundPacket.php:84-86`, `types/ServerJoinInformation.php:39-42`.
- A field not in `required` but with a `default` is written plainly: `Message` and `Parameter List` in `TextPacket.php:146-150`, `Parameters` in `CommandOutputPacket.php:62-64`. Bools of that shape are plain bools: `Allow aim assist` (`ClientCameraAimAssistPacketPayload.json:18-23`) at `ClientCameraAimAssistPacket.php:50`, `loadFromJson` (`CameraInstruction__SplineInstruction.json:17-22`) at `types/camera/CameraSplineInstruction.php:89`, and `DepthTest`, `ShowBackface`, `ShowTextBackface` (`TextDataPayload.json`, only `Text` required) at `types/shape/PrimitiveShapeTextPayload.php:58-60`. `/codegen` does not know this rule and drafts such a field as optional.
- An enum without `Enum-as-Value` has no single encoding in the package:
  - int32 enums of that shape are read as the enum's string name: `operation` at `types/AttributeValueBool.php:51`, `Version` at `CommandRequestPacket.php:46`, `Output Type` at `CommandOutputPacket.php:44`, `FOV Ease Type` at `types/camera/CameraFovInstruction.php:42`. No package read contradicts this.
  - uint8 enums of that shape are read as a string too: `Swing Source` (`ActorSwingSource`, `AnimatePacketPayload.json:34-38`) at `AnimatePacket.php:53`, `CloseReason` (`DataDrivenScreenClosedReason`) at `ServerboundDataDrivenScreenClosedPacket.php:44`, and `Playback Type` (`AudioContentPlaybackType`, `ClientboundPlayAudioContentPacketPayload.json:18-21`) at `ClientboundPlayAudioContentPacket.php:61`, by the steward's ruling on #15 (unit j). The schemas carry no other signal: each enum has `x-enum-binary-value`, and the field has `x-underlying-type: uint8` and no `x-serialization-options`.
  - The string is the schema label lowercased: `ClearOverrides` is `clearoverrides` (`types/OverrideUpdateType.php`), `FacialHair` is `facialhair` (`types/skin/PersonaSkinPieceType.php`).
  - Developer note `r26u0/bug_1526431` says a missing `Enum-as-Value` trait changed `TextPacket`'s bytes, not what they became. `/codegen` drafts every such enum as a lowercased string (`tools/codegen/generate.js:258`, `:313`).
  - A new uint8 enum of this shape, with no package read of that same enum, is an open question for #5 (question 3).

Field order is `x-ordinal-index`. Schemas are in the docs cache, `%LOCALAPPDATA%\pmmp-updater\cache\mojang-docs\v1.26.70-preview.23\json\<Type>.json`, and `v1.26.70-preview.24\json\` for units i1 and i2.
